# Content reuse decision — 19 October 2026

We can browse and link to UKC normally. We do not have a verified licence to copy its route library, consensus star ratings, descriptions, photos or topos. UKC terms expressly prohibit text/data mining and scraping. Individual facts such as a route name or grade may lack copyright protection in isolation, but a compiled database can have separate protection; systematic extraction is not cleared by calling its entries facts or taking many small batches. A free app is not automatically exempt. Contributors should record their own knowledge or provide an explicitly reusable source, not transcribe a commercial guide.

UKC's user-content licence is not a reliable permanent open-data grant for this app: it is bound to the terms, may end when the author deletes content, and does not override the site's scraping prohibition. We will seek an explicit licence before any library import; no permission request has been sent.

Rockfax's app terms prohibit copying/modifying its content. A paid subscription gives reading access, not redistribution rights. Photos, route drawings and authored descriptions remain protected. Rockfax's website licence is personal/noncommercial and requires written permission for broader copying/derivatives. No Rockfax content is imported.

Sources checked:

- [Rockfax app terms](https://rockfax.digital/terms_and_conditions)
- [Rockfax website terms](https://rockfax.com/general/terms/)
- [UK Intellectual Property Office: database rights](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/sui-generis-database-rights)
- [Copyright and Rights in Databases Regulations, regulation 16](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/3032/regulation/16)

This is a practical reuse assessment, not a legal opinion on every conceivable isolated fact. The operational rule is to use an evidenced open licence, original observations or explicit permission. Published seed records retain source, author, licence, changes and content hashes. Community submissions require rights consent and human review; public photos reused by another contributor retain their original attribution.

Google sign-in avoids password management but does not remove personal-data duties. A stable Google identifier, public alias, optional photo and uploaded contributions can identify people. We minimize collection, keep private ticks local, strip uploaded photo metadata, provide deletion/export controls, and do not add tracking analytics. See the [ICO explanation of identifiers](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/personal-information-what-is-it/what-is-personal-data/what-are-identifiers-and-related-factors) and [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
